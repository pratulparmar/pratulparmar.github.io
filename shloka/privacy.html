Shloka – Privacy Policy

App: Shloka (com.parmarlabs.shloka) · Developer: Parmar Labs · Effective 16 October 2026

In short: Shloka does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you set in the app stays on your phone.
संक्षेप में: श्लोक ऐप आपकी कोई निजी जानकारी इकट्ठा नहीं करता, न किसी सर्वर पर रखता है, न बेचता या साझा करता है। कोई खाता, विज्ञापन या ट्रैकिंग नहीं। ऐप की सारी सेटिंग आपके फ़ोन में ही रहती है।

What stays on your phone

The app saves these only on your device, so it can work as you set it up:

  • your chosen language and display settings (pictures, schedule, which texts to show);
  • which shloka is on your screen now, and which ones were shown, so they don't repeat too soon;
  • if you have Premium and type it in, the name printed on your WhatsApp Status picture;
  • whether you have Premium (a cached copy of what Google Play reports).

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it. Android's own backup may include these settings if you have turned device backup on.

Wallpaper and notifications

Shloka sets your home and lock screen wallpaper on the phone. If you allow notifications, it shows a local reminder in the morning when a new shloka is ready to share. Notifications are created on the phone, not sent from a server.

Sharing to WhatsApp

When you tap the WhatsApp button, the app makes a picture of today's shloka and opens WhatsApp with it. Nothing is posted unless you choose to send it in WhatsApp. What you share is then handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy. To offer this button, the app checks whether WhatsApp is installed on the phone; that check stays on the phone.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card, UPI or bank details. The app only receives from Google Play whether Premium is active, to unlock its features. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy.

Internet

The app itself does not connect to any server of ours. All shlokas, meanings, pictures and fonts are inside the app. The only online part is Google Play's purchase system, which the Play Store app runs.

Children

Shloka is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to the developer email shown on Shloka's Google Play page ("App support" section).
